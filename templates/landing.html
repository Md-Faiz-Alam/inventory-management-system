WHAT IT DOES

One workspace for the whole stock lifecycle

Built with Django and PostgreSQL. Stock is changed only through one locked, audited service.

◈

Live analytics dashboard

KPI cards, 30-day stock flow, value by category and fastest-moving products, plus a low-stock alert bell.

⇅

Safe stock control

Receipts, issues, reversals and stocktakes with row locking, duplicate-submit protection and running balances.

⬇

Purchase orders

Raise POs to suppliers, receive deliveries in parts against them, and track outstanding quantities.

⬆

Sales orders & invoices

Draft, confirm and fulfil customer orders. Fulfilment issues stock atomically and produces a PDF invoice.

⚑

Roles & audit log

Admin, Manager and Staff roles, user management, login rate limiting and a searchable who-did-what history.

{ }

Reports & REST API

Filtered reports as HTML, CSV or PDF, and a documented JSON API with interactive Swagger docs.

HOW IT WORKS

From supplier to customer

  1. Add products with SKU, price and reorder level.
  2. Raise a purchase order to a supplier.
  3. Receive the delivery against the PO; stock goes up.
  4. Create and confirm a customer sales order.
  5. Fulfil it; stock goes down and the invoice is ready.
  6. Watch the dashboard, reports and audit log update.
